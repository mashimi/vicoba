- Specific goal/outcome: Replace unsalted SHA-256 PIN storage with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (100k iterations, hardware-calibrated, random salt per user). Format: `pbkdf2_sha256$<iterations>$<salt_hex>$<hash_hex>`.
- Any constraints or requirements: Must transparently verify legacy 64-hex plain-SHA256 hashes and upgrade them in-place on successful login (with audit entry). 4-digit PIN space means salt + slow KDF is mandatory. Verification must iterate active users since per-user salts prevent direct hash lookup.
- Expected behavior: New users get PBKDF2 hashes; legacy users are auto-upgraded at first successful login; wrong PINs rejected without leaking which user existed.
